Dave Borzillo

Real ideas.

Dave helps people in knowledge work find better ways of working — whether that's how to run a workshop, build a product backlog, or lead a team that doesn't depend on one hero to hold it together.

The ideas aren't complicated. The hard part is doing them consistently. That's what Dave's coaching, training, and writing are all about: simple principles that actually stick.

"I was waking up at 4am to answer calls no one else would answer. I thought that was dedication. It was actually a system failure — and I was the broken part."

That realization is what turned a decade of firefighting into a career of teaching others how to stop. Dave has been a Registered Scrum Trainer for over a decade, working with teams across industries to replace heroism with systems that work without a hero.
